## Overview

WealthWise is a personal finance web app that uses AI to reduce the manual work of tracking money. It was built as a team project.

## What it does

- **Receipt scanning.** Upload a photo of a receipt and vision AI fills in the transaction form for you.
- **Budgeting.** Track income and expenses by account and category.
- **Financial health score.** A single index summarizes saving ratio, expense ratio, and emergency fund, with recommendations on what to improve.
- **Finance chatbot.** An AI planner answers questions about your own spending data.
